Windows has plenty of download managers, but on the Mac the options are few and most feel dated. We built FastLoader so big downloads feel light on both: a modern look, and a workflow as simple as paste a link and click.

## Features

- **Multi-connection downloads**: one file is split across 1 to 32 connections (8 by default). Connections that finish early help the slow ones, so the last few percent never drag.
- **Pause and resume**: close the app or shut down your laptop, and the download picks up where it left off.
- **Resumes when the internet comes back**: a dropped connection does not fail the download. FastLoader keeps retrying on its own until it succeeds.
- **Replace expired links**: if the server rejects an old link, paste a new one and the part already downloaded is kept.
- **YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook videos**: paste a link and pick a quality up to 4K, or MP3. TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook videos are saved in one folder per account, with the caption.
- **Catch downloads from the browser**: extensions for Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Safari. Right-click a link and choose "Download with FastLoader".
- **Tidy by default**: files land in Video, Music, Documents, Compressed, Programs, or Images.
- Queue, speed limit, completion notifications, light and dark themes, English and Indonesian.
