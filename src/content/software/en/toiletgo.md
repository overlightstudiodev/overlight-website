You are out on the road, at the mall, or in a city you do not know, and you need to go. ToiletGo shows the nearest public toilets on a map and tells you which ones are clean, free, or accessible, based on reviews from people who have been there.

## Features

- **Nearest toilet, right away**: the card under the map always shows the closest toilet that matches your filters.
- **Quick filters**: nearest, free of charge, wheelchair access, baby changing, and more.
- **Clear ratings**: separate scores for cleanliness and facilities, plus amenities like tissue, soap, and clean water.
- **Community reviews and photos**: share your experience and upload a photo of how it looks today.
- **Add a toilet**: mark a new toilet that is not on the map yet.
- **Contributor badges**: level up and collect badges every time you help someone else.
- **Take me there**: one tap to start navigation.
