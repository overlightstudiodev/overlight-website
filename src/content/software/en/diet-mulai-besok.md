"Diet mulai besok" is Indonesian for "my diet starts tomorrow", the thing we all say when logging food feels like a chore. With this app you just take a photo of your plate, and **TIYAS**, the AI assistant inside the app, estimates the calories and nutrients for you.

## Features

- **Snap or type**: take a photo of your food, or just type what you ate.
- **Automatic calories and nutrients**: calorie, protein, carb, and fat estimates in seconds.
- **Local food database**: hundreds of Indonesian dishes, from nasi padang and seblak to palm sugar iced coffee.
- **Diary and daily target**: see how many calories you have left today and how close you are to your goal.
- **Activity**: log workouts and steps, connected to Health Connect.
- **Chat with TIYAS**: ask about menus, portions, or eating habits.

## Free, with optional Premium

The core features are free. Premium unlocks extra features, and you can try it free for 7 days.

## How to install

1. Open Google Play on your Android phone.
2. Search for **Diet Mulai Besok**, or tap the download button above.
3. Install it, set up a quick profile, and snap your first meal.
